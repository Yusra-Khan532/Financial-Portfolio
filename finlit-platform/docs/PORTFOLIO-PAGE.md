# Equity portfolio page (route /holdings)

Spec: the design "FinLit Equity portfolio redesign" and the handover pack `portfolio_page` (README, `portfolio_metrics.py`, samples). The page describes a list of shares from names only; it never advises.

- Engine: `backend/finlit/portfolio_page/metrics.py` is the pack's `portfolio_metrics.py` unchanged (formulas, thresholds, reading rules, `BANNED_WORDS` check). `inputs.py` builds its inputs from the site's stored data: last 105 complete weeks of adjusted weekly closes (price-MAX, last close of each ISO week placed on its Friday), NIFTY 50 closes on the same weeks, meta = name, Sector label (variant spellings merged as the screener does), AMFI size band from market-value rank (L/M/S, "U" when unknown) and market value. It also adds `first_k` (long lists: how the first 12 names moved, and what the rest added).
- Endpoint: `POST /api/portfolio/page` `{symbols}`. Any other field (quantity, amount, price, date ...) is a 422. Silver and Gold (Basic: 403 plan_required). Cached in Redis by sorted names + the unfinished ISO week (6 h), so a changed list or a new week is recomputed. Style tilt (FinLit Prism parts) is added after the cache for Gold and admins only, as on the Diversification card; Silver gets a locked block with the reason.
- One score on the site: the Diversification card (Compare page) now takes its score, its two parts and "moves like N" from this engine (`diversification/api.py::_one_score`); its pair, group, risk and market figures keep the card's own 156-week windows. Equity portfolio no longer embeds the card.
- Live figures: price, today's change and the plan's scores come from `POST /api/portfolio/overview` (Silver: FinLit IQ and pillars, Gold: Prism parts), exactly as before.
- Frontend: `frontend/src/portfolio/` (sections, `derive.ts` pure helpers, `words.ts` fixed words, `pf.css`), page `frontend/src/pages/Holdings.tsx`, FAQ `content/myPortfolioFaqs.ts`. Every sentence about the list comes from the server's `readings` and `summary`.
- Tests: `backend/tests/test_portfolio_page_metrics.py` (pack tests, both samples reproduced from the pack's CSVs, also through the site's input path), `test_portfolio_page_api.py`; `frontend/src/portfolio/*.test.ts(x)` (helpers, wording scan of the rendered page in both modes for 12 and 40 shares, ordinals). Browser check with the API mocked from the samples: `frontend/e2e/my_portfolio_page.mjs`.
